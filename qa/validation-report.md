## Limits

This is an internal design-system release. The checks are not a complete accessibility audit and do not approve campaign/product/legal copy. Live quote submission, actual email-client rendering, destination-platform overlays and 200% browser zoom remain consuming-project checks. The email starter is intentionally a layout pattern with local preview image sources. Original high-resolution artwork is preserved; optimise suitable derivatives in production. Publication permissions remain as documented in `brand/publication.md`.
