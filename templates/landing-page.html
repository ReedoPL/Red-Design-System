Questions you might have

What is life insurance?

Life insurance can pay a lump sum if you die during the policy term, subject to the policy’s terms and conditions.

How much cover should I consider?

Think about the commitments you want to cover, such as a mortgage and family living costs. The amount and length of cover are your choice.

What should I check before buying?

Check the cover amount, policy term, premium, exclusions and the information you need to provide. Read the insurer’s policy documents.
