# Red: compact design context

Use this with `colors_and_type.css`, `components/components.css`, `styles/base.css`, the relevant template and `assets/manifest.json`.

**Source rules:** Red red `#ce4b38`; warm cream background `#fdf3e6`; ink `#271d1d` for copy only. Vesper Libre regular 400 body and bold 700 headings/subheadings. Preserve supplied logo artwork. One mascot composition at most per page, including partial Red art and mascot-containing scenes. Original illustrations only; explicit publication permission is required by the PDF. Pink `#f9d5cb` and blue `#5d71ab` are restricted and excluded from default CSS.

**System defaults:** Cream dominates. Red gives emphasis. Use thin editorial rules, flat surfaces, clear hierarchy and comfortable space. No Protect Line Poppins/Lora/navy/pink styling, gradients, glassmorphism or generic SaaS cards. Sentence case and warm British English. Voice, spacing, type sizes and components are proposed defaults, not source-approved detail.

**Accessible use:** Ink on cream for all normal copy, including links. Red on cream is 4.08:1: large text only (at least 24px regular or 18.67px bold). Cream on red has the same limit. Default CTAs are 20px bold, 48px minimum target. Keep readable form labels, keyboard controls, textual errors and visible focus. Native details work without JS. Test mobile and desktop.

**Asset shortcuts:** `assets/logos/red-primary.png` on cream; `assets/logos/red-white.png` on red. `assets/mascot/red-doll-house.png` for family/home, `red-thinking.png` for explanation, `red-relaxed.png` for calm, `red-personal-service.png` for service. Exact dimensions and context warnings are in the manifest. Do not fabricate a missing pose. Use `alt=""` when decorative.

**Copy integrity:** No invented premiums, eligibility, savings, ratings, reviews, contact details, time promises, offers or legal statement. Do not say Red gives regulated advice without a verified basis. Do not imply cover is guaranteed. Mark missing content `[REQUIRES SOURCE: …]`. Templates are internal demos; form success must not claim a live request occurred.

**Handoff:** Reuse a template, run `npm run check`, review responsive layout and report any missing sources or publication dependencies. Full rules: `AGENTS.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `brand/provenance.md`, `brand/publication.md`.
