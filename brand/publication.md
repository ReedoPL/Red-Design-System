# Publication dependencies

Internal design-system construction and review are authorised by the request to build this repository. Continue useful work and produce a concrete result. Do not treat the source’s publication requirements as a reason to stop routine layout work.

Before a particular asset goes live, record:

| Dependency | Why | Evidence to attach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mascot publication permission | PDF page 7 requests explicit permission for all mascot use before publication | Approver, scope, asset ID and campaign/destination |
| Babies/bubbles permission | PDF page 9 explicitly requests permission before use | Approver, chosen composition and context |
| Restricted palette permission, if used | PDF page 4 restricts two secondary swatches | Approver and permitted role/context |
| Product and financial-promotion content | Templates have no current approved commercial/legal source | Approved copy and limitations for the actual product |
| Footer, privacy and contact routes | These are not provided by the brand PDF | Current approved statements and working destinations |
| Reviews, offers or claims, if present | Asset filenames are not evidence of current claims | Source, date, attribution and relevant terms |

If the user already supplied the relevant permission and scope in the session, use it; do not ask again. Otherwise deliver the completed internal design and identify the remaining dependency precisely. This repository does not grant permission on behalf of the brand owner or approve a financial promotion.
